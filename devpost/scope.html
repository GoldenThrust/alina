Scope · Proof of Concept

Alina

A camera-assisted, AI-native skincare journal that turns repeated scans into a personal history of skin changes, plain-language insights, and personalized recommendations.

The Unique Kernel

Skinwise is not a one-time skin analyzer. Its distinctive value is the continuous agentic loop: capture a scan, understand the current skin state, compare it with prior scans, explain what changed, and recommend the next useful action while preserving the user’s preferences and control.

Core User Journey

01 · ScanAlexa says “check my skin” or the user presses Scan.
02 · ValidateOpenCV checks face position, lighting, blur, and image quality.
03 · AnalyzeYouCam returns cosmetic skin attributes and indicators.
04 · CompareThe agent retrieves history and explains changes over time.
05 · ActRecommend a routine or begin confirmed PayPal checkout.

Who It Is For

Skincare enthusiasts

People who want detailed trends and comparisons across days, weeks, and months, and who are interested in tracking a routine, understanding why their skin changes, and receiving recommendations that reflect both skin needs and personal preferences.

What “Working” Looks Like

A user can start a scan from the dashboard or through Alexa, review current YouCam results, ask how the skin has changed since the previous scan, receive a plain-language explanation, and see a personalized recommendation.

The demo should show one real skin-analysis response, multiple saved scans, a historical comparison, an AI-generated explanation, and a recommendation informed by a small preference profile.

Proof-of-Concept Boundary

In scope

  • Simulated/test camera capture with a camera-agnostic interface
  • OpenCV face detection, alignment, quality checks, and best-frame selection
  • Real YouCam Skin Analysis API integration
  • Persistent scan history and trend comparisons
  • AI agent with tool calling and conversation memory
  • Dashboard, plain-language summaries, and personalized recommendations
  • Small user-preference profile and curated product catalog
  • Required Qloo product lookup with a controlled local catalog fallback
  • Add-to-routine and confirmed PayPal checkout request flows

Later

  • Live Ring or other camera integrations
  • Production Alexa+ voice orchestration
  • Product catalog, cart, inventory, and fulfillment
  • Proactive reminders and notifications
  • Household profiles and shared reports
  • Nutrition, wearable, weather, and calendar tools
  • Medical claims or diagnostic advice

Explicitly Cut

  • Real Ring hardware integration
  • Automated PayPal purchases
  • Full Alexa+ voice experience
  • Advanced commerce and inventory systems
  • Broad wellness tracking beyond skincare
  • A complete multi-device or multi-camera platform

Agentic Platform Vision

Every integration is a tool. The AI decides when to capture, analyze, retrieve history, compare, recommend, explain, and request confirmation. New tools can be added without redesigning the whole system.
