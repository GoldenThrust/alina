Product Requirements · Approved

Alina

An assistant-first skincare journal that turns repeated skin scans into clear, conversational progress and personalized guidance.

The Core Journey

01 · OpenSee a concise current understanding of your skin.
02 · ScanCapture and validate a face image with OpenCV.
03 · UnderstandReview the current score, evidence, and plain-language summary.
04 · CompareAsk Alina about changes, history, and recommendations.
05 · ActAdd a routine item or confirm a PayPal checkout request.

Home Screen

Assistant-first

The home screen leads with the current plain-language summary, supports it with a compact score and recent-scan evidence, and ends with a contextual next action.

History and recommendations remain available, but do not compete with the understanding of the user’s skin today.

First Use

When no scans exist, Alina explains that the first scan creates a personal skin baseline and previews the future value of tracking progress.

The primary action is Start Your First Skin Scan. The user then receives scan preparation guidance, camera capture, validation, and an initial result.

Confidence and Progress

Transparent by design

After one scan, Alina provides a current snapshot without claiming long-term trends. After two scans, it can describe limited differences. After four scans, it may present more specific trend-based insight.

The four-scan milestone is shown as progress, not a requirement or penalty. It updates from “1 of 4” to “2 of 4” and then continues as the user scans.

Recommendations

Alina provides one primary product or routine recommendation, a short explanation, a confidence or limitation message, and useful alternatives when appropriate.

Qloo is required for the MVP recommendation path and is the primary product-data source. The curated local catalog is a controlled fallback only when Qloo is unavailable, unauthenticated, unusable, or temporarily failing. The UI must disclose degraded mode and must not imply that live Qloo data was used.

Scan Failure and Retry

If capture fails, Alina names one specific issue and gives one concise correction. The user can retry without starting over.

Examples include an invisible face, insufficient light, or an image that is not clear enough.

History and Conversation

Recent scans

A compact timeline shows the latest scans, skin score, and directional changes. It can open the complete History view.

Conversation

The user can ask about improvement, changes, routines, or recommendations. Alina explains what is known, what is not known, and what more scans would clarify.

States and Boundaries

  • First use, first scan, and first scan complete
  • One or two scans with limited trend claims
  • Scan in progress, scan failed, and scan complete
  • History available and no available data
  • Payment request requiring explicit confirmation

Proof of Concept

The MVP includes camera-agnostic capture, OpenCV validation, real YouCam analysis, persistent history, AI conversation, recommendations, required Qloo personalization, add-to-routine, and confirmed PayPal checkout request flows.

Live Ring, real-time Alexa+, production payment completion, and broader wellness tracking are deferred. Qloo is required; the local catalog is only a controlled degraded-mode fallback.
